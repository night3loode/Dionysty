What it is

A weekend where people actually get to know each other.

One evening is a party. Two nights together is something else: enough time for strangers to become people you'll keep.

Arrive on Friday evening and stay until Sunday morning. We take a beautiful home for the weekend, dress every corner of it, and fill it with good music, good food and the kind of people you came to meet. Every guest becomes part of this House for good.

A House is an event DIONYSTY hosts. Once you've been, you belong to it.

The six vibes

Every corner of the house has a purpose

A Vibe is what's happening in a space. Each one is made for a different way of meeting people.

The Foyer

Where you arrive and someone introduces you. Nobody starts the night alone.

The Salon

Where we talk, in small open circles you can step into.

The Card Room

Where we play: games, dares and the question of the night.

The Snug

A quiet, candlelit corner for twos and threes.

The Floor

Where we dance. Moving to the same beat brings strangers close.

The Supper Table

The long table, with seats that change between courses.

Your place

How to be on the guest list

  1. Say hello to Starlove

    Starlove is our AI guide, in Telegram. It asks your name, what you're here for, and how you'd come.

  2. A short call

    Ten minutes on a video call, so we know you're real and you know we are.

  3. The members say yes

    Our members decide who joins, in a private vote. A member's vouch counts as a yes.

  4. Your invitation

    We send you the details, the address and your door pass. The pass changes every 30 seconds, so it only works for you.

For now, men join through a member's vouch. Women, non-binary people and couples can say hello directly.

Care, always

How we look after the house

  • Adults only. Your age is checked at the door.
  • Consent comes first, every time, for everything. The Charter applies all weekend.
  • Sleeping rooms are for sleep and lock from the inside.
  • A sober host is awake through both nights and knows who's where.
  • A bed to rest in safely is never charged for.
  • Leaving is always easy, and no one asks why.
