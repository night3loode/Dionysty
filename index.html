The Charter

The terms we all keep.

is an adults-only, sex-positive community. This Charter sets out the conditions under which we gather. It applies to every member and guest, and to the founders and hosts exactly as much as anyone else.

The seven principles

IConsent

Every person keeps authority over their body, boundaries and participation. Ask before you touch. A yes to one thing is not a yes to another, and it can be withdrawn at any moment. Silence, hesitation or intoxication is never consent.

IIRespect

Dignity for every person regardless of body, gender, orientation, relationship status, background or experience. Harassment, intimidation and retaliation are not tolerated.

IIIDiscretion

Who you meet, what you see and what is shared stays inside the club. It's never gossip, never screenshots, never public. Discretion has limits only where someone's safety or the law requires action.

IVReciprocity

The community is sustained by generosity, consideration and contribution, which takes many forms. No one owes anyone intimacy, attention or constant availability.

VOpenness

Desire, curiosity and difference are welcome here without judgement. Openness is never a licence to pressure, persuade or shame.

VIAccountability

Concerns are heard through a clear process. Responses are proportionate, recorded where appropriate and open to review.

VIIAutonomy

Membership is voluntary. No member owes loyalty, exclusivity, money, personal disclosure, intimacy or continued participation. Anyone may leave without punishment.

 Adults only

Every member and guest is 18 or older, confirmed in person before attending any gathering.

At every gathering
  • Participation in any activity, conversation or moment is voluntary, and anyone can decline or stop without explaining why.
  • No phones or photographs in gathering spaces. No exceptions for hosts.
  • No one may pressure another person into intimacy, disclosure, spending, alcohol or any substance.
  • Someone too intoxicated to decide clearly cannot consent, and hosts may ask them to pause or leave.
  • Names, contact details, images and anything said in confidence never leave their agreed context.
  • Membership creates no exclusivity in your relationships, social life or other affiliations.
  • Concerns go to the designated host or contact, privately, during the evening or after it.
Concerns & accountability

A concern can be raised privately with a host or the founders, at any time. If it involves a founder, a separate trusted contact is available.

Every report is acknowledged, handled discreetly and assessed fairly. Interim steps to keep people safe may be taken straight away. Breaking the consent principle ends membership. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited.

Membership

How someone joins.

  1. i

    Introduce yourself

    Tell us a little about yourself and what draws you to something like this. There's no form, just a reply to whoever sent you this page.

  2. ii

    A real conversation

    We talk, then arrange a call if it feels right on both sides. You ask as many questions as we do: about the club, the people, privacy and the rules.

  3. iii

    The Antechamber

    With your agreement, you join a private Telegram channel for updates and invitations. Being there isn't membership yet.

  4. iv

    Meet in person

    A social evening in Delhi NCR: drinks, conversation, no expectations. It's for both sides to see whether this feels right.

  5. v

    The invitation

    If it's mutual, you're invited to join, agree to the Charter explicitly, and enter the members' private space.

Questions

Before you reply.

Do I have to do anything sexual?+

No. Participation in anything is entirely your choice, every time. Plenty of evenings are simply about the company, the setting and the conversation.

Is this a dating service?+

No. It's a community of people who share an openness and a set of values. Connections happen, but no one is here to be matched or obliged to anyone.

How is my privacy protected?+

Only what's needed is collected, it's kept privately, and you're never added to any channel without your yes. No photos at gatherings, and no names leave the club. You can ask for your details to be deleted at any time.

What does it cost?+

Any cost is stated clearly before you commit to anything, whether that's membership or a particular evening.

What if something goes wrong?+

Tell a host privately, at the event or afterwards. Every concern is heard, and if it involves the founders, there is a separate person to go to. Breaking the consent rules ends membership.

Can I leave?+

Any time, without explanation and without consequence.
