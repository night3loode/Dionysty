Membership

So, how does someone get in?

We start with a conversation. We take the rest seriously because everyone in the room deserves to feel they belong there.

  1. Say hello to Starlove.

    Start a private Telegram chat. Choose an alias, what you’d like to explore, how you’d come, and add a member code if someone invited you. No long form.

  2. Let’s have a call.

    Ten minutes over video. Tell us about yourself. Ask anything about the people, the privacy, the rules or the cost.

  3. The village has a say.

    Current members vote privately. More yes than no, with a majority taking part, means you’re in. A member’s vouch counts as a yes.

  4. Your Sigil lights up.

    Agree to our Charter, enter the private member space, and get your door pass. You’ll have a say in who joins next.

  5. Come to a House.

    We’ll invite you to your first gathering. The private address arrives close to the date, and a host will welcome you in.

A small thing to know. For now, men join through an existing member’s vouch. Women, non-binary people and couples can say hello directly.

Your Sigil

A little symbol that’s entirely yours.

When you apply, you place ten points across the seven Temples. Together, they make your seven-pointed Sigil, a quiet way of showing what you’re drawn to.

It starts unlit while your application is considered. Once you’re welcomed in, it lights up. Inside the village, your alias and Sigil are how people know you. Your real name doesn’t become part of anyone’s public story.

The Charter

Good company comes with good boundaries.

We want everyone here to feel safe enough to be themselves. So the rules are simple, serious, and the same for members, founders, hosts and guests.

  • IConsentAsk before touch. A yes can always become a no. Silence, hesitation or intoxication is not consent.
  • IIRespectNo harassment, discrimination, intimidation or retaliation. Every person deserves dignity.
  • IIIDiscretionNo public names, gossip, photos or screenshots. What people share stays private, except where safety or law requires otherwise.
  • IVReciprocityBring consideration and generosity. Nobody owes anyone attention, company or intimacy.
  • VOpennessDifferent people and ideas are welcome. Curiosity is never an excuse to pressure or shame anyone.
  • VIAccountabilityConcerns are heard privately and reviewed fairly. Hosts intervene when safety is at risk.
  • VIIAutonomyEvery choice is yours. No required loyalty, disclosure, spending, exclusivity or participation. You can leave at any time.
  • 18+Adults only18+. Government ID is checked in person before the first gathering. No ID copies or numbers are kept.

Safeguards

The practical things matter, too.

You should be able to ask the awkward questions before you step into a room. Here are some answers, in plain English.

Are members adults?

Everyone must be at least 18. A government ID is shown in person before attending a first House. We record only that the check happened, not an ID copy or number. Anyone under 18 is turned away.

Who's responsible for safety?

There are named hosts at every gathering, including a sober host who is awake and reachable. They can step in when consent or safety is at risk. There's also a separate trusted contact for concerns about a founder. We document action as appropriate. A serious consent breach ends membership.

How does payment work?

The contribution is the same regardless of gender, and you'll know it before committing. It covers things like the venue, food, artists and staff. Nobody is paid for anyone's company or intimacy, and sexual services are never bought or sold here.

What about alcohol and substances?

Alcohol is served only where the venue and required permissions allow it. There are no unlicensed sales and no illegal drugs. Nobody is pressured to drink. Hosts step in when someone is too intoxicated to give consent.

Where are the gatherings?

We meet in private venues and homes with the owner's knowledge of what's taking place. The exact address is shared only with confirmed guests, close to the gathering.

Can anyone take photos?

No. Phones are put away on arrival and nobody, including founders or hosts, photographs people in gathering spaces. We never publish members' names, faces or attendance. Website imagery is illustration, not member or event photography.

What information do you keep?

Only what's needed to run membership: your alias, Telegram ID or handle, optional Reddit handle, gender and how you'd attend, Temples and Sigil, member or vouch code, call time, application status, short answers and messages. Members voting see your alias, Temples, two answers, how you're attending, and who vouched, but not your Telegram ID or private messages. Votes remain anonymous. Members can message by alias through the bot; relay messages aren't retained unless part of a safety report. We don't ask for sexual orientation, health details, intimate preferences or ID numbers. Information is protected and deleted when you leave, are declined, or request it. You can use /forget in the bot.

What if authorities need information?

We cooperate with lawful requests and do not obstruct investigations. Privacy does not mean covering up harm. Age checks, consent processes and appropriate incident records support accountability.

Questions

You might be wondering…

We probably would be, too.

What actually happens at a House?

Music, sometimes a live artist, good food, games, long conversations, and people worth meeting. Hosts make introductions and help small groups settle in. The First House is a Friday-to-Sunday weekend.

Do I have to join every activity?

Of course not. Sit something out. Spend an hour talking. Come for the music, the art, the ideas or simply the company. Everything is voluntary.

Is this a dating thing?

It isn't a dating app or a matchmaking service. You meet people in groups and get to know them over time. Connections of all kinds can happen, but nothing is promised or expected.

Why do you use Telegram?

You apply on our website and use Telegram to talk privately with Starlove, get call details and invitations, and message another member through their alias. Your phone number and handle aren't shown to other members. Your Sigil, member card and door pass live in the app.

Will anyone know I'm a member?

We do not publish members' names, faces, attendance or personal details. There are no public member galleries. Inside the club, discretion is part of the Charter: people don't share one another's identity or private conversations.

How much does it cost?

We'll tell you the contribution for a House before you commit, and we'll happily discuss costs on your call. There are no surprises at the door.

What if something doesn't feel right?

Tell a host or founder privately at any time. If your concern is about a founder, there's a separate trusted contact. We review concerns discreetly and take action where needed. You won't be punished for speaking up.

Can I leave?

Anytime, without an explanation. You don't owe the club loyalty, exclusivity or a goodbye speech. Ask us to remove your information, or use /forget in the bot.
